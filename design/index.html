HelperHaven — two directions

Same product, same information architecture, two different emotional registers. Open each, scroll through the three screens, pick what feels right. It’s fine to say “A’s colour, B’s typography” — that’s the whole point of this exercise.

Direction A

Warm & Community

Sage + cream + terracotta. Fraunces serif headlines. Generous radii, soft shadows, photography-forward. Feels like Airbnb meets a good neighbourhood service.

View direction →
Direction B

Professional Concierge

Navy + ivory + muted gold. Playfair Display headlines. Restrained radii, thin borders, iconography-forward. Feels like a private-bank retail app or a premium recruiter.

View direction →

What to react to

  • Colour — warm sage vs. deep navy. Which signals trust to Singapore employers?
  • Typography — Fraunces (friendly serif) vs. Playfair (elegant serif) for headlines.
  • Density — A is airy, B is packed. Helpers probably prefer A. Employers may prefer B.
  • Privacy gate — compare how the blurred-helper card is treated. This is our visual signature.
  • Skill builder — both use 0–100 sliders with mastery labels, but the pacing differs.

Mockups are static HTML + Tailwind. Mix-and-match freely. If you want a hybrid of both, tell me which bits to keep.
