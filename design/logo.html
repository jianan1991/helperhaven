★ Chosen mark

The Cottage

A little house with a person inside. Warm, unambiguous, and right on the brand promise — we’re helping someone find a home in Singapore, not just an account on a platform.

1
A house is a home
The pitched roof reads instantly as “home” in every culture. We’re not an office, a spreadsheet, or an agency — we’re the place where a helper finds her next home.
2
Someone’s already inside
The clay figure isn’t approaching the door — she’s already in. Subtle but important: you’re not screening candidates, you’re welcoming someone.
3
Instantly understood
House + person. No decoding needed. An elderly employer, a first-time family, a helper at home in Indonesia — they all read it the same way.

Usage

Clearspace & don’ts

Give it room to breathe

Minimum clearspace = half the mark’s height on every side. Don’t crowd it with other elements.

dashed line = minimum clearspace
Don’t do these

The palette is the brand. Off-brand colour is the fastest way to look fake.

✗ Don’t recolour
✗ Don’t squash or stretch
✗ Don’t add effects or drop shadows
✗ Don’t put primary mark on clay — use the reverse variant
